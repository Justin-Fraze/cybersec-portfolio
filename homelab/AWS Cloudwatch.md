# AWS Cloudwatch Walkthrough

## Introduction

Moving infrastructure to Amazon Web Services (AWS) unlocks incredible scalability and flexibility, but managing virtual resources means losing physical visibility. Without a dashboard to watch your systems, it is impossible to catch performance bottlenecks or failures before they impact your applications.

This repository walks through building a hands-on home lab that leverages Amazon CloudWatch as your central command center. By tracking real-time performance metrics, aggregating system logs, and configuring automated alarms, you will learn how to monitor, troubleshoot, and maintain resilient cloud resources from the ground up.

## Objective

> To configure and deploy a foundational AWS home lab environment utilizing Amazon CloudWatch to collect system metrics, aggregate operational logs, and establish automated alarms for proactive resource monitoring.
